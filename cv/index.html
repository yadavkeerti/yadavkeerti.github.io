CV

Professional Summary

Recent Ph.D. graduate in Regional Development with experience collaborating across interdisciplinary research teams and with academic and professional partners. Experience spanning spatial data analysis, transit and mobility data, and quasi-experimental causal inference for urban infrastructure and transport policy. Doctoral research applies staggered Difference-in-Differences (Callaway-Sant’Anna) methodology to estimate the heterogeneous regional effects of high-speed rail across 31 South Korean cities (2008-2020), building and validating a multi-source panel dataset that integrates administrative records, road-network infrastructure, and satellite-derived (Landsat/Sentinel-2 NDBI) land-use change parameters. Current work involves developing and applying geospatial and deep learning approaches to climate-risk assessment, environmental and geophysical data interpretation, and urban and transportation-related research, while collaborating with researchers across interdisciplinary fields. Overall research experience spans urban infrastructure, transport, regional development, and Geospatial AI, with a focus on applying spatial data and quantitative methods to real-world urban and regional challenges.
