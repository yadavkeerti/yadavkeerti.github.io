An independently published book arguing that modern technology -- artificial light, electromagnetic fields, processed water -- disrupts mitochondrial and cellular health, and that reconnecting with natural rhythms is key to preventing chronic disease. Self-published and outside mainstream medical consensus; some of its specific claims have been flagged by reviewers as unscientific.
