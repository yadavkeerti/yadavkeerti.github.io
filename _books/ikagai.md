A study of the Japanese concept of *ikigai* -- one's reason for being -- drawn from interviews with residents of Okinawa, one of the world's longest-living populations, exploring how purpose, community, and steady activity contribute to a long life.

> Everyone has an ikigai -- a reason for living.
