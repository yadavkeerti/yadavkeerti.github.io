A foundational text of Jungian psychology, arguing that beneath each person's individual unconscious lies a deeper, shared layer inherited across humanity -- populated by universal symbols and patterns Jung called archetypes.

> "The archetype is essentially an unconscious content that is altered by becoming conscious."
