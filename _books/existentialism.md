Kierkegaard's foundational exploration of what it means to exist as a free individual — anxiety, choice, and despair aren't problems to eliminate but the necessary conditions of becoming a true self. He argues that authentic selfhood requires facing life's uncertainty and paradox head-on, often through a passionate "leap of faith" that reason alone can't justify.

> "Anxiety is the dizziness of freedom."
